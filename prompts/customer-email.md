# Customer email voice

Deterministic code in `lib/copy/emails.ts` writes every customer Savings Scan / Savings Plan Gmail draft. Editorial rewrite, recovery, PDF regeneration, and admin Recover all call that same builder. Do not invent a second email prompt.

This is a style and structure guide, not a Mad Lib. Mountains, findings, savings, page count, Stripe URL, and offer mode stay dynamic from verified research.

## Voice

Write like Ben, a Vermont ski dad who actually did the research and then fired off a quick email.

Use spoken cadence: contractions, "Looks like...", "I found...", "worth a look", "save some $$", "$49 bucks". Small natural variation from the family's facts is good. Do not manufacture typos, misspellings, or ambiguous grammar.

Do not sound like AI copy, SaaS lifecycle marketing, a sales funnel, a consulting summary, or corporate customer service.

## Mountains vs programs

Mountain/resort mentions use the name a local skier would say: Bolton, Burke, Dartmouth, Killington, Cochran's, Mad River Glen.

Official discount or program names stay recognizable when they help: Bolton Weekday Family Day, Killington's "Vermont Resident" Weekdays.

## SCAN_UPSELL shape

Hey {firstName},

I put together what I found around {mountains} to help save some $$ this season.

Looks like {1-2 verified high-level findings}.

I found a few solid options that are worth a look, roughly {firm savings} savings.

I attached your Savings Scan here.

I also put together a full {page count}-page Savings Plan with the exact programs, who qualifies, deadlines, fine print, blackouts, direct links, etc.

Get the full Savings Plan for $49
{stripe url}

And genuinely, if you get it and don't think it was worth $49 bucks, just reply and tell me. I'll refund it and you keep the Plan. No hoops or nonsense.

Ben

ps. This is a new project so any feedback you're willing to share would be incredibly helpful! Do these work/fit for you? Did you already know about them? If you could wave a magic wand, what would make booking skiing for your family easier/cheaper?

The Plan is work already done, not a teaser. Never use unlock, reveal, claim your savings, upgrade now, or "savings waiting for you."

Savings language stays uncertain. Do not say they will save the number. Only use firm counted savings from verified research.

Mention 1-2 promising examples. Do not dump eligibility rules, URLs, deadlines, blackouts, or enough detail to reconstruct the Plan.

## FULL_PLAN_FREE shape

Same voice and mountain names. Attach the Plan. Do not include a Stripe CTA, $49 pitch, refund guarantee, or any suggestion they need to buy the Plan.

## Paid fulfillment

After someone buys, thank them, attach the Plan, and keep the human refund line. Never add another checkout CTA.
